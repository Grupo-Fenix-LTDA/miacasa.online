Small atelier for ceramic, glass and wood pieces made to live in real homes.

We craft, engrave, and finish decor objects by hand — a slow, careful practice built on materials that age well and hold meaning over time.

The Atelier

A quiet studio in Jardim Trussardi, São Paulo.

L

LEANDRO DA SILVA MONTEIRO opened its doors in April 2016 as a small workshop dedicated to decorative pieces for the home. What began with a single ceramic kiln grew into a compact atelier serving families, interior designers, and independent stores across São Paulo.

We work directly with the materials — clay, glass, hardwood, and semi-precious surfaces such as marble and slate — finishing every piece with hand engraving, glazing, or polishing. Nothing leaves the studio without being inspected by the person who made it.

The atelier remains intentionally small. This is how we keep the work honest, and how our customers keep receiving pieces that feel personal rather than mass-produced.

What We Make

Four material families, one careful hand.

Every piece is finished at the atelier in São Paulo. Custom commissions are welcomed for interior design projects and gift orders.

Ceramic & Porcelain

Vases, bowls, planters, and table objects — hand-thrown, glazed, and fired at the studio.

Glass & Crystal

Engraved decorative pieces, crystal accents for shelves, and lapidary work by request.

Wood & Natural Fibers

Small artefacts in solid wood, cork, and woven natural fibers — trays, coasters, and mantle pieces.

Stone & Bijoux Objects

Polished marble and slate details, engraved plates, and small decorative objects in natural stone.

Our Process

Every piece passes through the same four steps.

A slow rhythm on purpose. This is how we keep the work honest and the studio small.

i

Material Selection

Clay, glass, hardwood, or natural stone — chosen by hand from local suppliers.

ii

Studio Craft

Shaped, thrown, or cut at the atelier — no outsourced production.

iii

Finishing

Glazing, engraving, polishing, or hand-lettering, according to the piece.

iv

Inspection & Pack

Reviewed by the maker, wrapped by hand, and shipped from São Paulo.
