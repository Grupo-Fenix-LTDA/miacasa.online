Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LEANDRO DA SILVA MONTEIRO (CNPJ 24.635.739/0001-38), a company registered in São Paulo, Brazil, collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information provided through the website miacasa.online. By using our website, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Who We Are

LEANDRO DA SILVA MONTEIRO is a small atelier based in São Paulo, Brazil, dedicated to handcrafted decor pieces in ceramic, glass, wood, and natural stone. Our contact details are provided at the end of this document.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary for us to respond to your inquiries or fulfill an order. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Full name and preferred contact name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Shipping and billing address (when applicable to an order)
  • Details about your commission or product request
  • Basic technical information (browser type, IP, and pages visited) collected automatically for security and analytics purposes

We do not collect payment card data directly. Payments, when applicable, are processed by third-party providers under their own security policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to messages, quotes, or commission requests
  • To process and ship orders
  • To send occasional updates about new pieces (only when you have expressly requested it)
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations under Brazilian law
  • To keep our website secure and functional

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Shipping partners, strictly for delivery purposes
  • Payment processors, strictly to complete transactions
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, when required by Brazilian law

5. Data Storage and Security

All personal data collected is stored on secure servers with access restricted to authorized personnel of the atelier. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain data only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes described above or as required by applicable law.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados — LGPD, Federal Law 13.709/2018), you have the right to:

  • Confirm the existence of processing of your personal data
  • Access your data and request corrections
  • Request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary data
  • Request the portability of your data to another provider
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website uses only strictly necessary cookies for site functionality and basic analytics. No cookies are used for advertising or third-party tracking without your consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
